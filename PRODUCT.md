# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite

## Users

Developer teams building SaaS products. Their primary job is to organize and move project work through delivery while keeping priorities and milestones visible.

## Product Purpose

Setu is a SaaS project-management product centered on a Kanban board. It should help teams manage tasks with less administrative effort. Success means teams spend materially less time updating and reconciling their project-management system.

## Positioning

Setu simplifies project management through one focused operating view rather than scattering status, priority, and milestone context across multiple screens.

## Operating Context

Teams work with tasks represented as cards on a Kanban board. They move cards between status columns, organize work into priority rows, and map tasks to milestones.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Kanban board with status columns.
- Movable task cards.
- Priority-based rows.
- Tasks can be mapped to milestones.
- No deployment target or browser matrix has been decided yet.

## Evidence on Hand

No existing product assets, proof, customer evidence, or implementation are available yet. Future work must not fabricate testimonials, customers, benchmarks, or performance claims.

## Product Principles

- Keep the core workflow in one focused view.
- Make status, priority, and milestone relationships immediately legible.
- Reduce project-management upkeep rather than adding process.
- Favor direct manipulation and clear task movement.
- Keep the first product scope centered on the confirmed Kanban workflow.
